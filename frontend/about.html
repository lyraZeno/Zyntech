Our Story

ZYNTECH started with a simple frustration every PC builder knows too well — ordering parts online and hoping they actually fit together. We built our Real-Time Compatibility Engine to take that guesswork out of the equation, checking socket types, RAM standards, and power requirements before a single order ships.

Today, we're a hardware store built by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts, committed to helping first-time builders and veterans alike put together systems that just work — with fast, trackable island-wide delivery and real hardware knowledge behind every recommendation.
