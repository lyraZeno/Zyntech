Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register or place an order on Zyntech, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details. If you choose to pay by card, payment details are collected only to confirm your order and are not stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To provide order tracking and customer support
  • To communicate important updates about your purchases

3. Data Storage

This is a demonstration front-end application. Account, cart and order data are stored locally in your browser's storage and are not transmitted to or stored on any external server.

4. Cookies

We use local browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember your cart between visits. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

5. Your Rights

You may update your profile details at any time from your Account page, or request that your data be removed by contacting our support team via the Contact Us page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
